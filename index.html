How it plays

Three picks. Two options. One winner.

Every round is the same short loop, and every round the armies get bigger. You never move a unit - the whole game is in what you draft.

1

Draft

Three picks per round. Each pick offers two units - take one. Reroll a pair for 20 coins, rising by 10 each time you do it again in the same round.

2

Counter

Counters are explicit, not guesswork. Assassins tear through ranged lines, Crossbowmen punch through Guardians, Knights hold off Assassins.

3

Battle

Both armies reveal, then fight on their own. No controls, no speed toggle - if the draft was right, you already won.

4

Conquer

The loser drops a life. Armies carry over in full and grow every round, so a bad early read follows you all match. First to zero lives loses.

The roster

Six units, one counter table

A small roster on purpose: every unit is worth drafting in the right matchup, and the counters are printed, not hidden.

Melee

Knight

The balanced line-holder. Nothing spectacular, never a wasted pick, and hard on Assassins.

Melee

Guardian Elite

A slow wall with little damage of its own. Buys the time your ranged units need - until a Crossbowman shows up.

Melee

Assassin

Fast and fragile. Holds a flank, then blinks behind the enemy line and deletes the archers.

Ranged

Archer

Long reach, steady damage, almost no health. Devastating behind a wall, useless in front of one.

Ranged

Spear Thrower

Shorter range than an Archer, heavier hits, slower throws. The mid-line compromise.

Ranged

Crossbowman Elite

Long range, brutal single-target damage, slow reload. The designated answer to anything armoured.

Campaign

Seven territories, seven ways to be wrong

Each territory rewrites one rule before you draft, and every one ends with a boss and a Hero. The terrain is shown before you commit - adapting to it is the whole point.

  • KingdomNo modifier. Where you learn the loop.
  • Greenwood ForestRanged and fast units are 10% more effective.
  • Iron MountainsHeavy melee gains 15% defence.
  • Ashen VolcanoAll damage up 20%. Battles end fast.
  • Frozen LandsEverything moves 15% slower.
  • ShadowlandsOne enemy draft pick stays hidden from you.
  • Crystal RealmRerolls cost half. Coins flow faster.

Clear the Crystal Realm to open the Sky Spire - an endless climb where every floor is generated from its own number.
